# Vision

## Mission

"The application exists to reduce financial stress by giving every unit of money a clear purpose while requiring less than one minute of attention per day."

## Problem

Existing budgeting tools are often too complex, require too much discipline, or focus on accounting instead of decision making.

The goal is not to replace accounting software. The goal is to answer one simple question:

"Can I safely spend this money today?"

## Target User

Each FIN installation is built for one owner and one installation security principal; it does not divide financial data between users. Product language and workflows should still be immediately understandable to other people who dislike budgeting.

## Product Goal

The application should:

- make daily money decisions effortless,
- answer daily spending with one conservative safe-to-spend number,
- protect reserved and savings money from being perceived as available,
- provide confidence instead of guilt,
- require minimal maintenance,
- encourage planning without feeling like accounting.

## Success Criteria

A successful user should be able to:

- open the app in under 30 seconds,
- immediately understand how much is safe to spend today,
- record an expense in under 10 seconds,
- close the app feeling more confident than before opening it.
